❔ HOW TO PLAY

W A S D Move your character around the arena.
MOUSE Aim. Your knife flies where the cursor points.
CLICK / SPACE Throw a knife. Cooldown between throws.
🔪 / 🤠 MODE Pick MURDERER (kill every innocent) or SHERIFF (find & shoot the red Murderer, protect innocents) before time runs out.
GOAL Murderer: kill all innocents. Sheriff: catch the Murderer, keep innocents alive.
⚠ CONE In either mode, whoever holds the gold cone sees in it. Get spotted (as murderer) or miss (as sheriff) at your peril. Crates are cover.
🪙 COINS Earned per kill + win bonus. Spend them on knife skins and maps in the shop.
P Pause · M Mute
